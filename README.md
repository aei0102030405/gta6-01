# Solmar Bay — an open-world crime game

Solmar Bay is a sun-soaked coastal city you can explore on foot and by car, built for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)'s gta6-01 bounty. It runs in a desktop browser from plain static files — no network, no build step, just keyboard and mouse.

You play Rico Vale, a driver for hire. Steal cars off the street and drive them, outrun the police when your wanted level climbs, take jobs from your fixer Marla, and earn your way across town. Traffic fills the roads, pedestrians walk the sidewalks, and the cops respond to everything you do.

What is in the game:

- A full city grid to explore — streets, blocks, buildings, a park, and a beach along the south shore, all drawn in a warm dusk palette.
- Driving and walking — run on foot with WASD and Shift, hop into any car with E, steer with the same keys and brake with Space.
- Car theft and traffic — parked cars and moving traffic to take, with drivers who flee when you pull them out.
- Wanted levels and police — one to five stars for stealing, hitting people, and shooting. Police cars spawn, chase, and ram you; lose them to cool the heat off.
- Combat — a pistol (F or mouse click to fire) and a punch (G) for close work. Police can be disabled, and your health regens when you lay low.
- Missions and a story — three jobs from Marla with objectives, dialogue, a map marker, and a timer: steal a car for the garage, run a package across town, and shake a two-star wanted level. Each pays out in cash.
- HUD and minimap — health, money, wanted stars, speed, the current objective, and a live minimap showing streets, police, and the mission marker.
- Save and sound — progress saves to the browser (money and finished jobs), and every effect is synthesized live with WebAudio: engine hum, sirens, gunshots, crashes. M mutes.

Run it with `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080
Controls:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Move / drive |
| Shift | Run |
| E | Enter or exit a car |
| F / click | Shoot |
| G | Punch |
| Space | Brake |
| P | Pause |
| M | Mute |
| L | Save progress |
